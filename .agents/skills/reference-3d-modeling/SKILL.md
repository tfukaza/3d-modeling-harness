# Reference-driven 3D modeling

Preserve the requested subject, style, deliverable and scope. Blender is the primary modeling tool; the editable `.blend` is the canonical model source. Keep each project's collected references, editable schematics, Blender source, authoring scripts when used, renders and review records together. Export additional formats as requested.

For substantial creation, follow this sequence in the [source-to-model workflow](references/reference-modeling.md):

1. Inspect the supplied reference and actively collect useful distinct references. Prioritize the exact model/variant for a specific target; for a generic subject, gather similar examples and define a coherent design. Search broadly until key views/details are covered and new searches mainly repeat evidence. Build and inspect a source-only packet with `scripts/build-reference-review.py`.
2. Identify [difficult regions](references/photo-contour-tracing.md#prioritize-difficult-regions) needing focused sketching/review. Where transparency, overlap or unclear interfaces obscure surfaces, first make and review [component photo traces](references/photo-contour-tracing.md) anchored to original pixels. For complex forms and assemblies, then generate a [color-coded structural study](references/generated-structure-studies.md) from an informative original view before schematics. Preserve camera and visible geometry, and keep it a reviewed interpretation. Skip simple/already clear geometry; record a skip if image generation is unavailable.
3. Delegate detailed schematic drawing to an author subagent when available, following the [drawing standard](references/schematic-workflow.md). Have independent critics assess geometry/proportions/detail coverage and construction/interfaces. Iterate with composited evidence until supported actionable discrepancies are resolved and relevant details have review status. Do not begin dependent production modeling with unresolved critical schematic geometry.
4. Delegate Blender modeling to a builder subagent when available. Before production geometry, record a component-level [construction plan](references/mesh-construction.md): surface continuity, modeling methods/modifier order, local edge treatments, openings and mating surfaces. Inspect a representative difficult component before repeating the method. Iterate proportion studies, detailed geometry and materials with independent model critics. For complex production geometry include a dedicated [topology review](references/evaluation.md#topology-review) of the saved cage, evaluated mesh and clay diagnostics; repeat it on the final frozen revision.
5. Verify the final saved `.blend`, relevant motion states and requested exports in their intended renderer/runtime. Report gaps and remaining discrepancies explicitly.

The coordinator assigns work, maintains coverage and issue/revision records, reconciles findings against originals, and controls stage transitions. Follow [independent review and staged prompts](references/independent-review.md) and [reviewer-owned contour comparison](references/paired-contour-review.md): critics inspect every retained original, account for per-source coverage, and independently trace corresponding source/render boundaries after matching camera and state. Overview inclusion and clean meshes do not establish source fidelity. Use complementary critics for substantial work; schedule topology, appearance or motion passes sequentially if slots are limited. If delegation is unavailable, retain the stages and disclose reduced independence. Simple edits and existing-model repairs may reuse current references, reviewed schematics and construction plans, rechecking affected relationships rather than restarting unrelated work.

Use `scripts/build-comparison-review.py` for paginated schematic/model-plus-reference boards and detail sheets; `scripts/compare-model-reference.py` remains useful for individual pairs. See [comparison usage](../../../scripts/model-comparison.md). Personally inspect generated boards and originals: view labels, generated hypotheses and numeric checks do not prove source fidelity. For mechanical subjects read [assembly modeling](references/assembly-modeling.md). Apply project-specific art direction only when requested.
