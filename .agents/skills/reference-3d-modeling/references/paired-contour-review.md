# Reviewer-owned contour comparison

A comparison board, plausible silhouette, clean mesh or agreement with the builder
does not establish that the model depicts the photographed surfaces. Reviewers
perform independent source interpretation, view matching and contour checks with
the same care as the author.

## Account for the references

Personally inspect every retained original. Maintain a compact per-source ledger
with source ID/hash, visible components/panels, candidate view and frozen revision,
camera/state status, reviewer-owned trace paths, findings and review status.
Each geometry-informing distinct source needs detailed comparison of the boundaries
it informed. An overview thumbnail or listed comparison is not completed review.
Complementary reviewers may divide component scopes; the coordinator audits their
combined coverage and uncovered relationships.

For a generic or deliberately stylized brief, compare the selected borrowed
features and declared departures. Do not require one design to match incompatible
specimens or undo the user's chosen style to obtain coincident contours.

Link duplicate-pixel sources to the inspected original. Record unrelated subjects,
variant differences, measurement-only evidence and other exclusions with specific
reasons. Measurement images still require their dimensional check. Difficulty
matching a camera is not grounds to exclude inconvenient evidence. An image with
no visible evidence for a component cannot support that component's acceptance.

## Establish correspondence before alignment

Save source-only observations and uncertainty without model images, author traces,
implementation or earlier pass claims. Map object axes, stable landmarks, visible
surfaces and state. For transparent parts distinguish their own boundaries from
transmitted edges, reflections and occluders. Label user-supplied information.

Render the frozen model from the corresponding side and state. Fit the camera
using distributed stable body landmarks outside the feature being tested, then
lock it and test held-out panel/component boundaries. Record camera parameters,
landmarks and remaining projection uncertainty. Do not tune the camera or geometry
solely to hide a disputed local mismatch. Do not mirror, stretch or warp images to
manufacture agreement; uniform framing/crop transforms are recorded.

Unmatched or uncalibrated views remain diagnostic. Seek a better render or another
source when they cannot settle a consequential relationship. Critical alignment
cannot pass using only unmatched views. Do not promise literal pixel identity
across lens distortion, blur, pose changes or uncertain boundaries.

## Draw both sides independently

Author editable native-coordinate SVG traces over the unchanged original and the
actual frozen-model render. Follow [photo contour tracing](photo-contour-tracing.md)
for layers, provenance, stable path IDs and photo-only/line-only inspection.
Reviewers create these observations themselves; copied builder traces, automatic
edge maps and mesh wireframes are not independent contour evidence.

Trace corresponding visible silhouettes, panel edges, cutout rims, junctions,
shape transitions and occlusion boundaries. Name the surface each line bounds and
retain surrounding material. Use corresponding semantic edge IDs; a reflection
and physical seam are not equivalent merely because both appear as lines. Do not
invent invisible edges to close contours. Separate uncertain/inferred segments.

Audit surface ownership in both directions: map each geometry-defining source
panel/component to an actual candidate surface patch, and each visible candidate
addition to source evidence or explicit user authority. An outline or isolated
strut does not prove that a broad panel's surface exists. Use disposable isolated
material masks, opaque surface views or sections to verify retained areas and
depth ordering on the frozen mesh; keep those diagnostics separate from raw
appearance comparisons. A transmitted patch or plausible hidden-fit explanation
does not establish a new visible component. Unresolved consequential ownership
is an incomplete review, not permission to accept invented hardware.

When a source-defining feature depends on protrusion, recess depth or coplanarity,
a frontal outline alone is insufficient. Inspect a grazing/alternate view and
compare its actual candidate surface to the neighboring reference plane with a
section or geometric measurement. Record unresolved source depth separately from
whether the chosen model implements an explicitly requested height relationship.

Inspect raw images, individual overlays and both line-only views. For matched
views inspect contrasting-color combined contours. Record landmark/edge residuals
in native pixels or relative to a shared body landmark, with source uncertainty
established before seeing the candidate. Residuals beyond that uncertainty require
investigation, not a tolerance widened afterward. Numeric distances supplement
surface ownership; they never automatically accept an asset.

## Evidence and signoff

Export transparent native-size PNG contour layers alongside editable SVGs. Use
paired contour inputs in the [comparison tools](../../../../scripts/model-comparison.md).
Apply the identical transform to image and contour. Retain raw originals/renders
and full unrotated context. Hash frozen model, renders, traces and inputs;
regenerate affected evidence after repairs.

Each match claim needs inspected source/candidate paths and actual boundary
evidence, not a checkbox. Findings identify source/candidate regions or path IDs,
consequence and repair goal. Check panel coverage, retained areas and interfaces
across other relevant images. Inspect actual saved mesh/sections for unresolved
depth or ownership. A schematic is another claim, not authority over the photos.

Signoff requires accounted reference coverage, reviewer-owned paired contours for
geometry-informing views, resolved supported discrepancies and explicit limits.
Unexamined critical regions or unresolved critical camera/ownership questions keep
review incomplete. Maintain the separate actual-mesh topology/assembly pass;
contour agreement and numerical cleanliness do not replace each other.
